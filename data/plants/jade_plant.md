## Light
Needs bright light and enjoys a few hours of direct sun.

## Water
Water when the soil is completely dry, about every 2 to 3 weeks in summer and monthly in winter.

## Soil
A cactus or succulent mix.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs if eaten.

## Common problems
Shrivelled leaves mean it is too dry. Leaves dropping and soft stems mean overwatering.

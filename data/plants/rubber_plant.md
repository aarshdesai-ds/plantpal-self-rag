## Light
Prefers bright, indirect light. Darker leaf varieties tolerate medium light.

## Water
Water when the top 3 to 5 cm of soil is dry.

## Soil
A free-draining houseplant mix.

## Feeding
Feed every 4 weeks in spring and summer with a balanced liquid fertiliser.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs. The sap can irritate skin.

## Common problems
Lower leaves dropping means too little light or a cold draught.

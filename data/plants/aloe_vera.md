## Light
Needs bright light and tolerates a few hours of direct sun a day.

## Water
Water deeply only when the soil is completely dry, about every 3 weeks.

## Soil
A gritty cactus mix in a pot with drainage holes.

## Feeding
Feed 2 to 3 times a year in spring and summer with a cactus fertiliser.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs if eaten.

## Common problems
Thin, pale, stretched leaves mean too little light. Soft brown leaves mean overwatering.

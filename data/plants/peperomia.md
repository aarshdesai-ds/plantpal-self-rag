## Light
Medium to bright, indirect light.

## Water
Water when the top half of the soil is dry. The fleshy leaves store water.

## Soil
A free-draining mix with perlite.

## Feeding
Feed every 6 weeks in spring and summer at half strength.

## Pets
Non-toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Drooping, soft stems mean overwatering.

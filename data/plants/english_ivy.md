## Light
Medium to bright, indirect light.

## Water
Water when the top 2 cm of soil is dry.

## Soil
A standard houseplant potting mix.

## Feeding
Feed every 4 weeks in spring and summer.

## Pets
Toxic to pets.

## Common problems
Spider mites are common in warm, dry rooms.

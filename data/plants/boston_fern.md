## Light
Medium, indirect light. Direct sun burns the fronds.

## Water
Keep the soil evenly moist but never soggy. Mist often or stand it on a tray of wet pebbles.

## Soil
A peat-free, moisture-holding mix.

## Feeding
Feed monthly in spring and summer with a balanced liquid fertiliser at half strength.

## Pets
Non-toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Crispy brown fronds mean dry air or dry soil.

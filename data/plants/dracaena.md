## Light
Medium, indirect light. Tolerates lower light.

## Water
Water when the top 3 cm of soil is dry. Use filtered water if possible.

## Soil
A standard houseplant potting mix.

## Feeding
Feed every 6 weeks in spring and summer.

## Pets
Toxic to pets.

## Common problems
Brown leaf tips are usually caused by fluoride in tap water.

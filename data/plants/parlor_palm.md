## Light
Low to medium, indirect light. Direct sun yellows the fronds.

## Water
Water when the top 2 to 3 cm of soil is dry.

## Soil
A standard houseplant potting mix.

## Feeding
Feed every 6 weeks in spring and summer at half strength.

## Pets
Non-toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Brown frond tips mean dry air or underwatering.

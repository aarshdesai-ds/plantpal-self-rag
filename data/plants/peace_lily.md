## Light
Happy in low to medium, indirect light. Too little light stops it flowering.

## Water
Water when the top 2 cm of soil is dry. It droops dramatically when thirsty and recovers within hours of watering.

## Soil
A moisture-holding houseplant mix.

## Feeding
Feed every 6 weeks in spring and summer with a balanced liquid fertiliser at half strength.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Drooping leaves usually mean it needs water. Brown leaf tips mean tap water or dry air.

## Light
Bright, indirect light with a little gentle morning sun.

## Water
Water when the soil is dry and the pearls look slightly wrinkled.

## Soil
A gritty succulent mix in a shallow pot.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs if eaten.

## Common problems
Mushy pearls mean overwatering. Flat, shrivelled pearls mean it is too dry.

## Light
Needs lots of bright, indirect light. Does not like being moved.

## Water
Water when the top 5 cm of soil is dry, roughly once a week.

## Soil
A free-draining houseplant mix.

## Feeding
Feed monthly in spring and summer with a fertiliser made for fiddle leaf figs.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Dropping leaves after a move are a stress response. Brown spots in the middle of leaves mean overwatering.

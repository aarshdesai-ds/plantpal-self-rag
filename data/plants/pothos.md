## Light
Grows in low light, but variegated leaves stay brighter in medium, indirect light.

## Water
Water when the top 5 cm of soil is dry, about once a week in summer.

## Soil
A standard houseplant potting mix.

## Feeding
Feed with a balanced liquid fertiliser every 4 weeks from spring to early autumn.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs. Chewing the leaves irritates the mouth.

## Common problems
Yellow leaves mean overwatering. Long bare stems mean too little light.

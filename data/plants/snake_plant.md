## Light
Tolerates low light but grows faster in bright, indirect light. Avoid long hours of harsh afternoon sun.

## Water
Water only when the soil is completely dry, usually every 2 to 3 weeks. Water even less in winter.

## Soil
A free-draining cactus or succulent mix.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs if eaten.

## Common problems
Mushy leaves at the base mean overwatering. Wrinkled leaves mean it is too dry.

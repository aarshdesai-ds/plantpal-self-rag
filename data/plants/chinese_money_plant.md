## Light
Bright, indirect light. Turn the pot weekly so it grows evenly.

## Water
Water when the top half of the soil is dry, roughly once a week.

## Soil
A free-draining houseplant mix with perlite.

## Feeding
Feed monthly in spring and summer with a balanced liquid fertiliser.

## Pets
Non-toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Leaves curling into domes mean too much or too little light.

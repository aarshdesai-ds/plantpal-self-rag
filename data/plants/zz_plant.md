## Light
Copes with low light and fluorescent office light. Bright, indirect light gives faster growth.

## Water
Water when the soil is completely dry, roughly every 2 to 3 weeks. The thick roots store water.

## Soil
A free-draining houseplant mix with added perlite.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs if eaten. Wash your hands after handling it.

## Common problems
Yellow leaves usually mean overwatering. Leaning stems mean it needs more light.

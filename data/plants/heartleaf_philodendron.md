## Light
Prefers medium, indirect light. Direct sun scorches the leaves.

## Water
Water when the top 2 to 3 cm of soil is dry.

## Soil
A rich houseplant mix that holds some moisture.

## Feeding
Feed with a balanced liquid fertiliser every 6 weeks in spring and summer.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs if chewed.

## Common problems
Small new leaves and long gaps between them mean it needs more light.

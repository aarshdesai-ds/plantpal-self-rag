## Light
Bright, indirect light. Needs long, dark nights in autumn to set flower buds.

## Water
Water when the top 2 cm of soil is dry. Water less after flowering.

## Soil
A free-draining mix of potting soil and orchid bark.

## Pets
Non-toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Buds dropping means a sudden change in temperature or light.

## Light
Medium, indirect light. Direct sun fades the leaf patterns.

## Water
Keep the soil lightly moist. Use rainwater or filtered water if you can.

## Soil
A moisture-holding houseplant mix.

## Feeding
Feed monthly in spring and summer at half strength.

## Pets
Non-toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Curling leaves mean thirst or dry air. Crispy edges often come from hard tap water.

## Light
Prefers bright, indirect light. Tolerates medium light.

## Water
Water when the top 2 to 3 cm of soil is dry.

## Soil
A standard houseplant potting mix.

## Feeding
Feed every 4 weeks in spring and summer with a balanced liquid fertiliser.

## Pets
Non-toxic to cats and dogs.

## Common problems
Brown tips are common and usually caused by fluoride in tap water.

## Light
Needs bright, indirect light to grow split leaves. A metre from a sunny window is ideal.

## Water
Water when the top 5 cm of soil is dry, usually every 1 to 2 weeks.

## Soil
A chunky aroid mix with bark and perlite.

## Feeding
Feed monthly with a balanced liquid fertiliser in spring and summer. Do not feed in winter.

## Pets
Toxic to cats and dogs if eaten.

## Common problems
Leaves without splits mean too little light. Brown edges mean dry air or underwatering.
